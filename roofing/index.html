How to Estimate Roof Shingles

Roofing is unique because you cannot simply measure the "footprint" of your house. Because the roof is angled (pitched), the actual surface area is much larger than the ground area.

Understanding "Pitch" and "Squares"

What is a Square?

In roofing terms, 1 Square = 100 Square Feet . You buy materials by the square, not by the foot.

Bundles per Square

Standard shingles come in bundles. 3 Bundles = 1 Square . (So if you need 10 squares, you need 30 bundles).

The Pitch Multiplier

A flat roof (0/12) has a multiplier of 1. A steep roof (12/12 or 45 degrees) has a multiplier of 1.414.

  • 4/12 Pitch: Low slope. Easy to walk on.
  • 6/12 Pitch: Standard suburban home. Walkable with care.
  • 12/12 Pitch: Very steep. Requires safety harness and toe-boards.

Don't Forget the Overhangs

Most roofs extend past the walls of the house to protect the foundation from water. This is called the "Eave" or "Overhang." Our calculator adds this to your length and width automatically before calculating the pitch.

Architectural vs. 3-Tab Shingles

When you go to the lumber yard, you will be asked which type you want.

Type Pros Cons
3-Tab (Flat) Cheapest option. Lower wind rating (60mph). Lasts 15-20 years.
Architectural Looks 3D/Textured. High wind rating (110mph). Costs ~20% more. Heavier to carry.

Vital Accessories: Ice & Water Shield

If you live in a cold climate where it snows, Felt Paper is not enough.

You must install a self-sealing rubber membrane called Ice & Water Shield on the bottom 3 feet of the roof (the eaves). This prevents "Ice Dams" from backing water up under your shingles and rotting your wood.

Ventilation: Ridge Vents

A roof needs to breathe. If heat gets trapped in your attic, it will "cook" the shingles from the bottom up, cutting their lifespan in half.

  • Ridge Vent: A plastic vent installed at the very peak of the roof. It allows hot air to escape naturally.
  • Soffit Vents: Vents under the eaves that let cool air IN. You need both for the system to work.

⚠️ Safety Warning

Roofing is dangerous. If your roof pitch is 8/12 or higher , we strongly recommend hiring a professional. Falls from steep roofs are the #1 cause of injury in home construction.
