Dumpster Rental Guide: Sizes, Costs & "Heavy" Mistakes

Renting a roll-off dumpster seems simple until you get hit with an "Overage Fee" for weight or run out of space halfway through demolition. Choosing the right size (Cubic Yards) is an art form. This guide breaks down exactly what fits in a 10, 20, or 30-yard container so you can budget accurately.

1 Standard Dumpster Sizes Explained

10-Yard Dumpster

The "Small Driveway" Choice

Fits in a standard parking spot. Perfect for small bathroom remodels, garage cleanouts, or small deck removals.

  • • Capacity: ~3 Pickup Truck Loads
  • • Weight Limit: 1 - 2 Tons

20-Yard Dumpster

The Most Popular

The standard for kitchen remodels, roof replacements, and carpet removal for a whole house. Low sides make it easy to toss trash over.

  • • Capacity: ~6 Pickup Truck Loads
  • • Weight Limit: 2 - 3 Tons

2 The Heavy Debris Trap (Concrete & Dirt)

Volume vs. Weight

A 20-yard dumpster full of drywall weighs about 2 tons. A 20-yard dumpster full of concrete weighs 25 tons . The truck cannot lift it.

If you are disposing of "heavy materials" (Concrete, Dirt, Brick, or Roofing Shingles), you usually cannot use a large dumpster. Most rental companies will limit you to a 10-yard "Lowboy" container to keep the weight legal for road transport.

The Rule: Never fill a dumpster more than halfway with dirt or concrete.

3 What You CANNOT Throw Away

Throwing banned items in a dumpster will result in a hefty fine (often $50-$100 per item) because the landfill rejects the load.

Tires They trap gas/methane underground.
Paint / Liquids Paint cans must be dried out first.
Batteries Car batteries are hazardous waste.
Appliances Fridges contain Freon (requires fee).

Strategic Loading Tips

  • Break It Down: Don't throw a whole cabinet in. Smash it flat. Air pockets cost you money.
  • Load Flat First: Put plywood, doors, and drywall at the very bottom to create a flat floor.
  • Open the Gate: Walk heavy items in through the swinging back door. Don't try to throw a toilet over the 6-foot side wall.
