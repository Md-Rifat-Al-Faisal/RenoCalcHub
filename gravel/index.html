Gravel Buying Guide: #57 vs. Crusher Run

Ordering gravel is confusing because it is sold by weight (Tons), but you measure your driveway by volume (Cubic Yards).

The "Density Rule"

The Magic Number is 1.4

One Cubic Yard of standard gravel weighs approximately 1.4 Tons (2,800 lbs).
If you calculate 10 cubic yards of space, you need to order 14 Tons of stone. Our calculator handles this density conversion for you automatically based on the stone type.

Which Stone Should You Buy?

Crusher Run (Base Layer)

Also called "Process" or "21A". It contains stone dust and jagged rocks. It compacts into a hard, cement-like surface. Use this for the bottom layer.

#57 Stone (Top Layer)

Clean, crushed stone about the size of a golf ball. It allows water to drain through it. Use this for the top 2-3 inches to minimize mud.

Preventing Pot-Holes: The Crown

The #1 reason gravel driveways get potholes is standing water.

When grading your driveway, you must create a "Crown" in the center. The middle of the driveway should be 1-2 inches higher than the edges so water runs off into the grass. If the driveway is flat or bowl-shaped, water will sit, soften the ground, and create potholes.

Weed Fabric: Yes or No?

YES. Always lay heavy-duty Geotextile Fabric (Driveway Fabric) between the dirt and the gravel. If you don't, your expensive gravel will sink into the mud over time, and you will have to re-gravel every 2 years.
