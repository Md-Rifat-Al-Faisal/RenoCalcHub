Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 13, 2025

At RenoCalcHub.com, accessible from https://renocalchub.com, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors.

Log Files

RenoCalcHub follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, and referring/exit pages.

Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, RenoCalcHub uses "cookies". These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.
