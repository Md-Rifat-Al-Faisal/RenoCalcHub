Complete Tile Buying Guide: From Grout to Thinset

Tiling is one of the most expensive DIY mistakes you can make if you measure incorrectly. Unlike paint (where you can just buy another gallon), running out of tile is a disaster because dye lots change. If you buy a second batch later, the color might be slightly off.

Understanding Waste Factors

10% - Standard Grid

For standard square or rectangular rooms using a grid or brick pattern. This covers standard cuts at the wall edges.

20% - Diagonal Layout

If you rotate the tile 45 degrees (Diamond pattern), you must cut every single tile that touches a wall on two sides. The waste is massive.

Grout: Sanded vs. Unsanded

Choosing the wrong grout can scratch your tile or crack over time.

  • Unsanded Grout: Use for joints smaller than 1/8 inch . Crucial for polished marble or glass tile (sand would scratch the surface).
  • Sanded Grout: Use for joints 1/8 inch or larger . The sand acts as aggregate to prevent the grout from shrinking and cracking as it dries.
  • Epoxy Grout: Expensive and hard to apply, but 100% waterproof and stain-proof. Best for shower floors.

Which Trowel Size?

The size of your trowel notch determines how much mortar goes under the tile.

Tile Size Notch Size
Small (Mosaics) 3/16" V-Notch
Medium (12"x12") 1/4" x 1/4" Square Notch
Large (12"x24"+) 1/2" x 1/2" Square Notch

Subfloor Prep: Wood vs. Concrete

Never tile directly over plywood. The wood expands and contracts with humidity, which will crack your tile or grout lines.

  • On Wood Subfloors: You must install 1/4" or 1/2" Cement Backer Board first. Use thinset and screws to secure it.
  • On Concrete Slabs: You can often tile directly, but applying a "Crack Isolation Membrane" (a rubbery paint) is recommended to prevent existing foundation cracks from transferring to your tile.

Sealing Natural Stone

If you buy Marble, Travertine, or Slate , you must seal the tiles BEFORE you grout.

Natural stone is porous. If you spread dark grout over unsealed white marble, the stone will suck up the grout color and stain permanently grey.
