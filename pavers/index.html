Paver Installation Guide: The 3 Layers

A patio is only as strong as what is underneath it. If you just lay bricks on dirt, they will sink and wobble within a month. You must build the correct "Club Sandwich" of layers.

Layer 1: The Sub-Base (Gravel)

This is the structural foundation. You need Compactable Gravel (often called CR6, 21A, or Road Base). It is a mix of crushed stone and stone dust that packs down hard like cement.

  • Walkways: 4 inches deep.
  • Patios: 6 inches deep.
  • Driveways: 8-10 inches deep.

Layer 2: Bedding Sand

This is a 1-inch layer of clean concrete sand. Its only job is to provide a smooth, level bed for the pavers to sit on.

⚠️ Warning: Do not use more than 1 inch of sand! If the sand layer is too thick (like 2-3 inches), the pavers will shift and rock when you walk on them.

Layer 3: Polymeric Sand (The Glue)

Once the pavers are laid, you sweep Polymeric Sand into the joints. This special sand contains glue that hardens when you mist it with water. It locks the pavers together and prevents weeds and ants from coming through.

Why Compaction Matters

You cannot just shovel the gravel in. You must rent a Plate Compactor and run it over the gravel every 2 inches you add. If you skip this, your patio will settle and hold water puddles.
