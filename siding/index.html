Siding Buying Guide: Vinyl vs. Fiber Cement

Siding is the skin of your house. It protects against rain, wind, and pests. Calculating it requires thinking in "Squares" rather than simple feet.

What is a "Square"?

In the siding industry, materials are sold by the Square .

1 Square = 100 Square Feet (10' x 10' area).
If your wall is 400 sq ft, you need 4 Squares.

The "Hidden" Accessories

You can't just nail siding to the wall. You need a system of trims to hold it in place and make it watertight.

  • Starter Strip: The very first piece at the bottom of the wall. The first row of siding hooks into this.
  • J-Channel: Installed around every window and door. It hides the cut edges of the siding panels.
  • Corner Posts: Large vertical caps that cover the corners where two walls meet.
  • Undersill Trim: Used at the very top of the wall under the roof overhang (soffit).

House Wrap: The Vapor Barrier

Before you hang a single piece of siding, you must wrap the wood sheathing in a moisture barrier (like Tyvek).

Siding is not waterproof. Water will get behind it during storms. House wrap ensures that water drains down to the ground instead of soaking into your insulation and causing mold.
